Holding tabs

With no tabs the part comes loose during side B finishing. Use tabs unless the part is big and you hold it another way.

Lining up side B

Two holes are drilled on the flip line, through the stock and into the spoilboard. Dowels in those holes put side B back exactly where side A was. Measure your dowels: dowel rod is often a little under size. "Extra for fit" makes the hole that much bigger (0 = tight, 0.005 = slip fit).

No holes are drilled. Before cutting side A, screw two straight strips to the spoilboard touching the left and front edges of the board (or trace around it). Flipping across the center keeps the same footprint, so you put the board back into that spot and X/Y zero stays put. The board must be cut square and exactly the size in Stock, or the two halves won't line up.

More: margin and overlap

The carve pocket is the part's outline plus this room on every side; the rest of the board stays full thickness as a frame. Make the room at least as wide as your biggest bit.
