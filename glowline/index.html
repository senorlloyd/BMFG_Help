LED flex can't branch, cross itself or turn a sharp corner. GlowLine splits each word into runs: one unbroken piece of flex each. At the end of a run the flex goes down a hole and travels behind the board to the start of the next run.

Check your flex's spec sheet for its minimum bend radius. Spots tighter than that are circled in orange; make the sign bigger to open them up.

Grooves use your neon groove bit along each letter's centerline. Holes are cut separately with a straight bit, all the way through.

GlowLine starts each groove at a flex through-hole when one sits on it, so cutting the holes drills away the plunge mark. Any other plunge spots show as a small × in the flex plan.

Zero X and Y at the lower-left corner of the board and Z at the top of the material. Files are inch G-code saved as .sbp, the same as RouteReady. Always air-cut or preview in ShopBot before cutting.

How to build it

  1. Cut the grooves with the neon groove bit, then switch bits and cut the holes.
  2. Print the wiring map. Start at run 1: feed the flex up through the first hole, press it into the groove, and push it down the hole at the end of the run.
  3. On the back, carry the flex (or cut and solder wire) along the dashed line to the next number, and come back up.
  4. Snap the diffuser cap into the groove lip once every run is in.
