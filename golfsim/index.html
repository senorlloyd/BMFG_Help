        How it plays
        • Drag back from the ball and let go to putt. Drag anywhere else to orbit, scroll to zoom.
        • A part with tee in its name marks the start. One with cup or hole marks the hole. Everything else is solid.
        • Parts named spinner or windmill spin, around a part named hub if one sits on them. Add a speed like “spinner 20rpm”. Parts named slider or gate slide across the hole; add an angle like “slider 45deg” to turn the path. Parts named lift or drawbridge sink into the floor and rise again. Change any part under Moving parts.
        • Drop a PNG or JPG to put it on the flag. It also prints on top of any part named decal.
        • Open or drop a .golfhole file to play a hole someone shared.
        • Ball is 42.7 mm, cup is 108 mm, same for every hole.
        • Off the course costs one stroke. The hole ends after 8.
        • The clock starts on your first putt. Scores rank by fewest strokes, then fastest time, and are saved on this device.
