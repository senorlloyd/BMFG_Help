Meet WreSTL

Have you ever had a hard STL file that you needed to edit?

Maybe it's way too big for your printer. Maybe you only need one piece of it. WreSTL lets you grab a 3D model and wrestle it into shape, no CAD program needed.

  1. Open it. Drag an STL file onto the screen, or click Open STL.
  2. Size it. Make it bigger or smaller. Too big for your printer? WreSTL can shrink it to fit.
  3. Trim it. Use the Crop box to keep just one part, or Cut to slice it. The cut side gets filled in, so it still prints solid.
  4. Save it. Download your new STL and send it to your slicer like normal.

Got a broken file? WreSTL tries to patch holes and backward faces the moment you open it.
